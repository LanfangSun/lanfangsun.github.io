How I teach

Authentic learning

I design tasks around real language use — real audiences, real texts, and the digital and multilingual spaces learners already live in — so that what happens in class connects to how language is used beyond it.

Technology for self-directed learning

I use technology, including generative AI, to help learners become self-directed: setting their own goals, finding resources, and carrying their learning beyond the classroom through the digital tools and communities they already use. Along the way, they question these tools — what they get right, what they miss, and which judgements must stay their own.

Learner agency

I see learners as the agents of their own learning. Through peer review, self-assessment, and dialogic feedback, I invite them to make and justify their own choices, so they take ownership of how they learn and keep learning after the course ends.
