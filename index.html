About

Grew up by the sea in Yantai, Shandong — this site borrows its colours from that coast.

I hold a PhD in Education from the University of Hong Kong and am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Education University of Hong Kong, where I also serve as a Visiting Researcher.

My research lies at the intersection of technology and language education. My primary research interests include: (1) technology-enhanced second and foreign language learning; (2) informal and out-of-class language learning through digital and social media; (3) teacher education and professional development; and (4) instructional design for culturally and linguistically diverse learners.

My interest in language learning is also personal. Through informal learning and social media, I have learned a great deal from other languages, cultures, and communities beyond formal classrooms. These experiences continue to shape the questions I ask as a researcher and inspire me to explore how people learn languages through everyday interactions, digital platforms, and meaningful connections with others.

More broadly, I am interested in understanding how emerging technologies, digital platforms, and innovative pedagogies can support language learning, teacher development, and more inclusive educational experiences. I also hope to continue exploring languages and learning from people and communities around the world.

Research currents

See them on the publication map

My work maps onto two crossings: teachers and learners, formal and informal settings. Generative AI runs through all four like a current.

Teacher education

Pre-service teachers’ classroom-talk reflection with video visualisation, and instructional design for online Chinese and college English.

Teachers beyond the classroom

Edu-influencers on social media, and teachers learning alongside AI in online professional communities.

Classroom language learning

Chinese as a second language, and students’ academic and multimodal writing with generative AI.

Learning beyond the classroom

Vlog-assisted informal language learning and AI-mediated informal digital learning of English (AI-IDLE).
